## 2. Nature of the App

BioGuardian is a mobile application designed to assist users in identifying animal species through image-based recognition and provide information about their habitat, conservation status, and more. It also includes a blog feature allowing users to contribute and share knowledge about endangered species.

> **Disclaimer:**  
> This app is intended solely for **educational and informational purposes**. Species identification is **not guaranteed to be accurate**. Misidentifications may occur due to lighting, angle, or non-animal input.  
> **Users must upload only images of animals.** Submitting any image of humans, deities, or other non-animal subjects is strictly **prohibited** and may lead to account restriction or removal.

## 3. Data Collection and Use

### 3.1. User-Provided Data

- **Images:**  
When using the species recognition feature, users upload images. These images are used temporarily for analysis and are **not stored permanently** unless explicitly allowed.
  
- **Blog Content:**  
Posts submitted to the community blog are stored and moderated.

### 3.2. Device and Usage Data

- **Location Data:**  
Used for features like displaying nearby wildlife reserves or regional species. Collected only when explicitly allowed by the user.

- **Log Data:**  
May include device model, operating system, crash logs, and other diagnostics in case of app errors.

### 3.3. Third-Party Services

BioGuardian may use services such as:

- [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
